Hi, we're RUSH GRAFIKZ.
RG Crafts & Print Services, a small print and craft shop from Cagayan de Oro.

We make ready-to-print designs and templates so you can skip the hours of layout work and get straight to printing, crafting, and selling.

Whether you run a small button pin or sticker business, you're a parent or teacher prepping learning materials for the kids, or you just love making things, our files are built to be easy: print, cut, done.

Our Story | How it all started

Every business has a story, and this is ours. ❤️

We started this little journey back in June 2024, with nothing but an idea, a lot of curiosity, and the willingness to try something new.

It all began with digital templates—designs I personally created from scratch. I was never formally taught how to do most of these things. I was self-taught, learning as I went, figuring things out through endless tutorials, trial and error, mistakes, and sometimes, starting all over again.

Read our full story

It wasn't always easy. There were moments of doubt, frustrations, and times when I questioned whether my work was good enough. But I kept going. I learned that you don't have to know everything before you start. Sometimes, you just have to take that first step, be willing to learn, and trust the process.

Transitioning from the corporate world into the world of digital creativity and small business ownership was never an easy journey. It meant stepping outside my comfort zone, learning new skills, taking risks, and slowly building something of my own. It taught me that growth doesn't happen overnight, and that some of the best lessons come from the things that don't go as planned.

From being a digital creator, I eventually found myself exploring handmade crafts and printing. What started with just ONE printer slowly turned into something bigger. I began investing in printing materials, learning about different products, experimenting with ideas, and taking small orders whenever I could.

One printer became the beginning of our printing business era. Little by little, those small steps brought us somewhere I once only hoped to be—to finally having a shop of our own.

Looking back, it's amazing how far this little business has come. From creating digital designs at home, figuring things out one day at a time, to making physical products, fulfilling orders, and now having a space we can proudly call our own.

We're still learning. Still growing. Still making mistakes, discovering new things, and dreaming of what's next. But one thing remains the same: the passion to create something meaningful and the determination to keep moving forward.

To everyone who has supported us, purchased our products, downloaded our templates, shared our posts, recommended our little shop, and trusted our work—THANK YOU. You are all part of this journey, and we wouldn't be here without your support.

And above all, none of this would have been possible without God's grace, guidance, and blessings. All the hard work, all the little victories, and every milestone—we give it all back to Him.

To God be all the glory! 🙏❤️

This is our story. And we're just getting started.

Privacy Policy.

Effective November 13, 2026

RUSH GRAFIKZ (RG Crafts & Print Services) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you shop with us, why we need it, and how we keep it safe, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. What we collect

  • Order details: the items you buy, totals, and your order reference.
  • Contact details: your name, mobile number or Facebook name, and email address.
  • Delivery address: only for physical or custom orders.
  • Proof of payment: the screenshot you upload, which may show your account name, part of your account number, and the reference number.
  • Messages: anything you send us through Messenger, email, or our social pages.

We never ask for your card number, PIN, OTP, or e-wallet password. You pay inside your own GCash, Maya, MariBank, or banking app.

2. How we use it

  • To verify your payment and confirm your order.
  • To send your Google Drive download link by email.
  • To prepare, ship, or arrange pickup of physical orders.
  • To answer your questions and fix any issues with your files.
  • To keep sales records for our accounting and tax requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

Only with the tools and partners we need to run the shop:

  • Tally (our checkout form) and Airtable (our order records).
  • Google (Drive for file delivery and Gmail for sending your links).
  • Couriers and riders such as J&T and Maxim, who receive only your name, number, and address to deliver physical orders.

Some of these services store data on servers outside the Philippines.

4. Your cart and this website

Your cart is saved in your own browser so it's still there when you come back. It isn't sent to us until you check out. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Some pages load fonts from Google and the checkout form from Tally, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to complete your order, handle any follow-up concerns, and meet our accounting and legal obligations. After that, we delete it.

6. How we protect it

Only the people running RUSH GRAFIKZ can access your order details. We use reputable platforms and keep our accounts secured.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Just message us using the details below. If you believe your data privacy rights were violated, you may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date at the top.
